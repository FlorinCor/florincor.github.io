ODDLY Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

This policy explains how ODDLY, an Android game published by Florin Cordunianu, handles information when you use the app. If you have a question or a privacy request, contact cordunianuflorin@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

ODDLY stores your level progress, unlocked levels, scores, play mode and settings such as sound, haptics, contrast and reduced motion on your device so that the game can remember your choices. We do not operate an ODDLY account or cloud-save service.

The app asks you to choose an age range or skip the question. It keeps only a broad advertising category—adult, or child/unknown—on your device. It does not save your exact selected range or ask for your date of birth. The category determines whether the app can start its advertising and consent services.

You can reset game progress in ODDLY’s settings. Resetting progress does not reset all other app settings or the age category. You can remove the app’s locally stored data by clearing its storage in Android settings or uninstalling it; this also removes your locally saved progress.

Advertising and privacy choices

ODDLY includes Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). In the current version, players who select a range under 21, or skip the age question, are treated as child/unknown. The app does not call advertising SDK initialization, request ads or request a UMP consent message for that category. The game remains playable without ads.

For players in the adult category, the app uses UMP to check whether a privacy message is required and whether ads can be requested. UMP contacts Google to update privacy information and may do so even when no ad is displayed. Where applicable, you can make or change your privacy choices through Ad privacy options in the game’s settings. The option appears when Google’s consent system indicates that it is required. Depending on your location and choices, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. Non-personalized ads can still involve data used for purposes such as ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

When Google’s advertising services are used, Google may collect and share information including your IP address and approximate location derived from it; app and ad interactions such as launches, taps and video views; performance and diagnostic information; and device or account identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and app set ID where applicable. Google describes these practices in its Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure, privacy policy, and explanation of how it uses information from apps that use its services. Google and its ad partners may process data in countries other than the one where you live.

You can also reset or delete the Android advertising ID using your device’s Android settings, and manage Google advertising preferences through My Ad Center. The availability and effect of individual controls can depend on your device, region and Google account settings.

Children and families

ODDLY is intended for children and adults. As described above, the current version does not request ads or UMP consent messages for players classified as child/unknown. If these practices change, this policy will be updated. Parents or guardians with questions or requests about a child’s information can contact us at the address above.

Storage, retention and security

Game progress, settings and the broad age category are stored locally for as long as they remain on the device. ODDLY’s reset option removes progress; clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the app’s local data. We do not control how long Google or its ad partners retain data they process; see their privacy information linked above for details. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your rights and contact

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent. You may also have the right to contact your local data protection authority. To ask about the information handled through ODDLY, contact cordunianuflorin@gmail.com. Because game progress is stored on your device rather than in an ODDLY account, we may need additional information to understand and respond to your request; do not send sensitive information unless needed.

Visiting this policy page

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date at the top shows when the current policy took effect.
