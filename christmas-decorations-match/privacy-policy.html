Privacy Policy

Christmas Decorations Match
Effective date: 10 November 2026

This policy describes how Florin Cordunianu (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use Christmas Decorations Match for Android. Contact: cordunianuflorin@gmail.com.

Game data on your device

The game stores progress, levels, scores, lives, boosters and settings on your device. You do not need to create an account to play. Android backup, if enabled on your device, may back up and restore app data through your Google account under your device settings.

Age information

The game asks for a year of birth or allows you to continue without answering. The year is used on your device to decide whether to enable advertising; it is not saved or sent by the game. The game does not request ads or show the advertising consent form when the year indicates a player may be under 18 or when no year is given. You may be asked again when the game restarts.

Advertising and consent

The game uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform to display banner ads and manage applicable privacy choices. Google and its advertising partners may collect and process your IP address (which may indicate an approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, interactions with the app and ads, and diagnostic information. They use this information for advertising, measuring performance, analytics, security and fraud prevention. The exact processing depends on your device settings, location, consent choices and Google's services.

When required, a consent message is shown. Where available, you can revisit your choices through Settings → Privacy options. You can also manage or reset your advertising ID in Android settings. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Contact and deletion

If you contact us, we use the information in your message to respond and retain it as needed for support and applicable obligations. You can delete locally stored game data using Android's app settings or by uninstalling the game; backups, if enabled, may be retained separately under your Google account settings. For questions or requests concerning information we hold, email us at the address above. For data held by Google, consult Google's privacy controls and policy.

Changes

We may update this policy as the game or its services change. The effective date above identifies the current version.
