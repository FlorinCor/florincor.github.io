Tidy Up Privacy Policy

Effective November 18, 2026

This policy applies to Tidy Up, the Android puzzle game from Florin Cordunianu (SimpleGamesLabs).

Game progress and settings

You can play without creating an account or connecting to the internet. Tidy Up saves your current puzzle, piece positions, completed levels, and preferences on your device so you can resume play. The game does not send those records to the developer or to Google. They stay on your device until you clear Tidy Up’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app.

Ads and consent

Tidy Up uses Google AdMob to display banners and app-open ads. It uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to manage advertising consent. Advertising and consent messages can use an internet connection. Depending on your region, device settings, and privacy choices, Google and its advertising partners may process or receive information such as:

  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location.
  • App and ad interactions, such as launches, views, and taps.
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash and performance information.
  • Device or account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID or app set ID when available.

Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, provide analytics, and prevent fraud. Google says its Mobile Ads SDK encrypts this data in transit. Google’s privacy and retention policies explain how it handles and retains this information. See Google’s Mobile Ads SDK disclosure, Google’s Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google’s data retention policy.

Your privacy choices

  • Where Google requires an in-app consent control, open Settings and select Ad privacy options to review or change your advertising choices.
  • Use your Android privacy settings to manage or delete your advertising ID where supported.
  • Clear Tidy Up’s app storage or uninstall the game to remove local progress and preferences.

Support and privacy requests

If you email about the game, the developer uses your email address and anything you share to respond and resolve your request. Support correspondence is kept as long as needed for that purpose and to meet applicable legal obligations.

To ask a privacy question or request access to or deletion of information you shared with the developer, email cordunianuflorin@gmail.com.

Age range

Tidy Up is intended for players aged 13 and older. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has shared personal information with the developer, please use the contact address above.
