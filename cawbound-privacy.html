Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

SimpleGamesLabs (“we”, “us” or “our”) publishes Cawbound for Android (com.florin.crowbound). This policy explains how the game handles information. For privacy questions or requests, email cordunianuflorin@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Cawbound stores your best score, coin balance, extra lives and sound preference on your device so that they are available when you play again. The game does not ask you to create an account, and its game code does not send these saved values to a developer-operated server.

You can delete these local values by clearing Cawbound’s app storage in your device settings or uninstalling the app. This also deletes your saved progress. Installing a different package does not automatically transfer that progress.

Advertising and privacy choices

Cawbound uses Google AdMob to provide ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform to manage privacy choices where applicable. Google’s Mobile Ads SDK may collect and share:

  • Your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location.
  • Device and account identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID, app set ID and, where applicable, identifiers associated with accounts on your device.
  • Interactions, such as app launches, taps and ad views.
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time, performance and errors.

Google and its advertising partners may use this information to provide and measure ads, conduct analytics, prevent fraud and abuse, and, depending on your settings and choices, personalize ads. They may use device identifiers and similar technologies for these purposes. Information may be shared with Google and, depending on the advertising configuration and your choices, other advertising partners.

For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Where privacy options are required and available, you can use Settings → Privacy options in the game to review or change your choices. You can also manage or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings, where supported. Some processing may still occur for purposes such as providing non-personalized ads, security or complying with legal obligations.

Retention, deletion and security

Cawbound keeps the game values described above on your device until you clear the app’s storage or uninstall it. Google and any advertising partners handle their own information and retention according to their policies; see Google’s privacy information linked above for its retention and deletion practices.

Google states that data collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to request access to, correction or deletion of your personal information, or to object to or restrict certain processing. To contact us about a privacy request, email cordunianuflorin@gmail.com. You can also use the privacy controls and Google resources described above.

Audience

Cawbound is intended for people aged 13 and older. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern about a child’s use of the game, please contact us at cordunianuflorin@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the game or its services change. We will publish the updated policy on this page and revise the date above.
