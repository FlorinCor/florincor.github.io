Christmas Globe Drop for Android

Privacy policy

Effective November 18, 2026 · Developer: Cordunianu Florin

Christmas Globe Drop is a puzzle game for players aged 13 and older. You can play without an account. This policy covers the game, its advertising services and this page.

Your game and age choice

The game saves your round, best score, settings and age selection on your device. It asks for an age group rather than a date of birth. You can change your answer in Settings under Age group.

In version 1.0.4 and later, choosing “Under 18” or “I prefer not to say” applies the same protections. The app signals under-age treatment to Google's consent service and requests restricted, non-personalized ads with a maximum PG content rating. Google disables remarketing and the transmission of the Android advertising ID for these requests. Choosing “18 or older” lets Google's regional privacy choices govern eligible ads. Choosing an age group does not give consent to personalized advertising.

Who processes advertising data

The game uses Google AdMob to show app-open ads and Google's User Messaging Platform to manage privacy choices. Depending on your settings and eligibility, Google and its advertising partners may process an IP address, approximate location inferred from it, device and advertising identifiers, app launches, ad impressions, interactions, and performance or crash diagnostics. They use this information to deliver and measure ads, diagnose problems and prevent fraud. The applicable age and privacy signals accompany these requests. The developer can receive aggregate advertising reports. The game does not send your saved board or score to a developer server.

Google describes its processing, retention, transfers and available controls in How Google uses information from apps, the Google Privacy Policy and the AdMob age-treatment guide.

European and US privacy choices

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, eligible players can use Google's message to consent, decline or manage purposes and partners. In supported US states, eligible players can use the US privacy message to opt out of applicable sale, sharing or targeted advertising. Open Settings and choose Privacy options when available to review or change those choices. The game remains playable if you decline or an ad is unavailable.

Retention, deletion and your rights

Your local progress and settings remain until they are replaced, you clear the game's storage, or you uninstall it. Clearing local storage also removes your saved round. Google and its partners manage their own records under their respective policies. Android's advertising privacy settings provide additional controls.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, opt out or complain to a data protection authority. To ask about information the developer handles, email cordunianuflorin@gmail.com and mention Christmas Globe Drop. This also applies to requests from parents and guardians. If you contact the developer, your email address and message are used to respond to your request and retained as needed for that purpose.
