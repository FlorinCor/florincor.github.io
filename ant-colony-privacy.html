About the game

This policy explains how Ant Colony handles information when you play the Android game, use its advertising or privacy features, contact the developer, or visit this page. The game can be played offline and does not require an account, sign-in, or a developer-operated game server.

Information stored on your device

The game stores colony progress, a local recovery backup, tutorial progress, and settings such as sound, volume, and reduced motion in the app's private storage. These are used to run and restore the game. They are not sent to a developer-operated game server. Google may also store consent and advertising-related information on the device as described below.

The app is configured to disable Android automatic backup and exclude its data from Android device transfer; platform or device behavior can vary. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes local game data and settings, including the local recovery copy. This does not delete data already held independently by Google. The app does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or precise device location.

Advertising and consent

On Android, the game uses Google's Mobile Ads SDK and AdMob to request an app-open advertisement during initial loading. It does not request another advertisement during gameplay. An internet connection is needed for advertising and consent services, but the game can continue when an ad is unavailable.

Google may collect and share your IP address (which can be used to estimate an approximate location), app and ad interactions such as launches, taps, and video views, diagnostic or performance information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID or app set ID, where applicable. Google's consent message may also describe the use of precise geolocation data by advertising partners where available and permitted. Android advertising services may also be involved where available and permitted. Google and advertising technology providers may use this information to deliver and measure ads, provide advertising analytics, and prevent fraud and abuse. The developer may receive advertising reports from AdMob; the game does not add a separate analytics or crash-reporting service.

Depending on your choices and applicable requirements, advertising may be personalized, non-personalized, or limited. Even non-personalized or limited ads can involve some data processing for delivery and fraud prevention. See Google's privacy policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services, and Google's Mobile Ads data disclosure.

Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks consent requirements and, where required, can display a consent form. The service processes consent choices and related device or network information. The app requests ads only when UMP says ad requests are permitted; a timeout or failed request is not treated as consent.

Your privacy choices

Choose Settings → Privacy policy in the game to open this page. When Google's consent platform requires a privacy-options entry, Settings → Privacy choices lets you review or change the choices offered. Availability depends on Google, your location, and the publisher's consent configuration.

You can also review controls in Android's privacy or advertising settings, including resetting or deleting the advertising ID where offered, and Google My Ad Center. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing that was lawful before withdrawal. Controls for data independently held by Google are described in Google's privacy policy.

Support requests

If you email cordunianuflorin@gmail.com, the developer receives your email address and whatever you include in your message or attachments. This information is used to respond, investigate your request, and keep relevant support records. The developer's email provider handles the message to deliver and store it. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Retention, deletion, and rights

Local progress and settings remain on your device until removed through Android's app-storage controls, uninstalling the app, or otherwise deleting them. The developer cannot remotely retrieve or delete a save that remains only on your device. There is no game account to delete.

Support correspondence is kept as long as needed to handle your request, maintain relevant records, or meet applicable legal obligations. You can request deletion of correspondence by contacting the developer. Google sets retention periods for data it holds; see Google's retention information. Deleting local app data does not automatically delete information Google already holds.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data protection authority. Contact the developer about information you provided directly. For information held independently by Google, use Google's privacy controls or contact Google. Reasonable verification may be needed to handle a request.

Security and international processing

Local saves are held in Android app-private storage. Google states that information collected by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS. Email and other third-party services follow their providers' security practices; no storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.

Google, GitHub, and their service providers may process information outside your country. Their privacy policies explain the safeguards that apply to their services.

Children and parents

The game does not ask for a player's name, email address, age, or a game account to play. The advertising and consent processing described above can still occur. A parent or guardian who believes a child has sent personal information to the developer through support may contact the developer to request review and appropriate deletion.

This page and changes

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security; see the GitHub privacy statement. This policy page adds no analytics scripts, advertising, forms, or external fonts. Linked services have their own privacy practices.

The policy may change when the game or its data handling changes. The updated version and effective date will appear on this page. For privacy questions or requests, email cordunianuflorin@gmail.com and mention Ant Colony.
