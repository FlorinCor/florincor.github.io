Fish Bubble for Android

Privacy policy

Effective November 26, 2026

Cordunianu Florin publishes Fish Bubble. Its Android package is com.bubblepop.game.

You can play Fish Bubble offline without creating an account. This policy covers local game data, advertising and advertising choices, support inquiries, and visits to the public policy page.

Game data and age group

The game uses its private app storage to keep campaign progress, completed levels, saved attempts, and your age-group choice. The age screen offers “Under 18,” “18 or older,” and “I prefer not to say.” The game asks for a group rather than a date of birth. You can change your answer in Settings under Age group. Your saved board and progress are kept on your device, and the developer operates no game server to receive them.

The game waits for an age-group answer before requesting ads. “Under 18” and “I prefer not to say” apply the same protections. The game requests child age treatment and a maximum G (general audiences) content rating for their ads. Google disables personalized ads, remarketing, and transmission of the Android advertising ID for those requests. When the game checks privacy status with Google’s User Messaging Platform, it sends the under-age-of-consent signal for both choices. Players who choose “18 or older” receive ads subject to the privacy choices available in their region. See Google’s age treatment guidance for more information.

Advertising and consent services

Eligible adults may see a Google AdMob app-open ad when the game opens. AdMob may also display an interstitial between levels after every fifth distinct level cleared. Age treatment, ad availability, and privacy choices determine what appears. Gameplay is available whenever advertising is skipped or unavailable.

The game uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to check eligibility and present any applicable consent or opt-out form. Google can process device and network information and store privacy choices and signals so that advertising can respect them.

For advertising and consent, Google and participating advertising partners may process an IP address and approximate location derived from it, identifiers such as the Android advertising ID or app set ID where available, app and ad interactions, and performance or diagnostic information. This data supports delivering and measuring ads, advertising analytics, troubleshooting, and fraud prevention. The developer can receive aggregate ad reports. Google describes the SDK’s data handling, including encryption in transit, in its Android advertising data disclosure.

For details about processing, sharing, retention, and international transfers, read how Google uses information from apps and the Google Privacy Policy. Applicable regional messages identify participating partners and purposes.

European and US privacy choices

Eligible players in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland can use Google’s regional message to consent, decline, or manage purposes and partners. In supported US states, eligible players can use the US privacy message to opt out of applicable sale, sharing, or targeted advertising. Open Settings and select Privacy options when available to revisit the choices provided by Google, including withdrawing consent.

Depending on your choices and eligibility, Google may serve personalized, non-personalized, or limited ads. Non-personalized and limited ads can still involve data used to deliver or protect ads. You can also review controls in your Android advertising privacy settings and Google My Ad Center.

Support, retention and deletion

If you contact the developer, your email address, message, and any information you include are used to handle your request. The developer and the email service may retain the correspondence as needed for that purpose or to meet applicable obligations.

Local data is kept until it is replaced, you clear the app’s storage, or you uninstall the game, subject to Android’s storage behavior. Clearing storage also removes saved progress. Information already held by Google and its advertising partners follows their retention policies and available controls.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal information, object to or restrict processing, withdraw consent, or contact a data protection authority. For support information or other data the developer handles, email cordunianuflorin@gmail.com and mention Fish Bubble. Parents and guardians can use the same contact for privacy questions or requests.
