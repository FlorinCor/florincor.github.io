Gravity Courier · Android

Privacy policy

How the game stores your progress and how its advertising services handle data.

Effective and last updated: 6 November 2026

Who and what this policy covers

This policy covers Gravity Courier, Android package com.florin.gravitycourier, and this policy page. The game is provided by Florin Cordunianu (FlorinCor). For privacy questions, email cordunianuflorin@gmail.com.

Game progress on your device

Gravity Courier has no game account or game backend. It stores your completed missions, stars, best results, selected rocket, tutorial progress, settings and an in-progress mission in the app's private storage. A local recovery copy helps protect progress if a save is damaged. These records are used to run the game, restore a flight and remember your preferences; they are not sent to a developer game server. The app opts out of Android app backup.

Gameplay can be used offline. Google advertising and privacy messages use an internet connection. The game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. There is no separate developer-added gameplay analytics or crash-reporting service; Google's advertising services have their own data practices described below.

Google advertising and privacy choices

The game uses Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK to request a banner on the home screen. It does not show ads during a mission. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks privacy requirements and can display consent messages. The game requests ads only when UMP permits it. Availability and applicable choices depend on your region, device and the configured messages.

The game asks for a date of birth to select appropriate ad settings. The date itself is not saved or sent off the device. The app stores only an age category on the device. Players can skip the question and play without ad requests; the app also does not request ads for players who enter an age under 13. For ages 13–17, the app sends Google a child-treatment signal for ad requests and configures UMP for users under the age of consent. This signal communicates the relevant age category, not the date of birth. All ad requests use a general-audience content rating. The age category can be changed in Settings or removed by clearing app storage.

When Google's advertising services are used, Google may collect and share:

  • IP address, which may indicate an approximate location;
  • app and ad interactions, including launches, taps and video views;
  • app and SDK diagnostic or performance information;
  • device and account identifiers, such as an app-set ID and, where applicable, identifiers associated with signed-in accounts; and
  • the child-treatment setting described above, when applicable.

Google describes these data as used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google and its advertising partners process information under their own policies and the applicable privacy choices; the developer may receive aggregate advertising reports. The Mobile Ads SDK uses TLS to encrypt data in transit according to Google. Limited or non-personalized ads can still involve data processing.

The app does not request permission to access the Android advertising ID or Android Privacy Sandbox ad signals. When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, it appears under Settings → Privacy options in the game. Withdrawing a choice affects future processing that depends on it and does not erase information already processed.

Read Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps using its services and Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Retention, deletion and security

Local game records remain until changed or removed. To remove them, use Android Settings → Apps → Gravity Courier → Storage → Clear storage or uninstall the game. This removes local progress, settings, recovery files and locally stored consent records; the app may request privacy choices again if reopened. Device menu names may differ.

There is no game account to delete and the developer has no server copy of your game save. Clearing the app does not delete information already processed by Google or correspondence sent to the developer. Google's retention and deletion practices are described in its privacy policy and privacy controls. Google may process data in other countries. Local records rely on Android's app-private storage protections; no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Contact, rights and children

If you contact the developer, your email address, message and information you choose to provide are used to respond and handle your request. Email providers process that correspondence. Messages are retained as needed to resolve the request and maintain necessary support or legal records, then removed when no longer needed. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, object to or restrict processing, or withdraw consent. Contact the developer at cordunianuflorin@gmail.com about information handled directly by the developer, and use Google's privacy controls for information held by Google. You may also contact your local data-protection authority. A parent or guardian with a privacy concern may use the same contact address.
