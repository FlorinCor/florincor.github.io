Privacy Policy

Santa’s Tree Workshop for Android
Developer: Florin Cordunianu (SimpleGamesLabs)

Effective and last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy explains how Santa’s Tree Workshop handles information, including when children play. Contact us at cordunianuflorin@gmail.com.

Game data on your device

Your tree layouts, progress, settings and album images are stored on your device. The game does not require an account or send these creations to a game server. Removing the app or clearing its app data deletes its local game data. Images you explicitly export or share are handled by the Android destination or recipient you choose and can remain outside the app after uninstalling.

Age and privacy choices

The game asks you to select an age group to choose an appropriate privacy flow. You may choose not to say. The game reduces the selection to a broad adult, minor or unknown category and stores only that category on the device; it does not ask for a date of birth or send the selection to a game server. Players who choose not to say are treated as under age for this flow. Clearing the app’s data removes that choice.

Where a privacy choice is required and available, Google’s User Messaging Platform may present it. If a privacy-options button is shown in Settings, you can use it to review your choices. The app tags every ad request for child treatment, regardless of the age category.

Advertising and service providers

The game may show one Google AdMob interstitial after Santa finishes reviewing a tree. The game can be played when an ad is unavailable. Ad requests are configured for child treatment and a general-audience maximum content rating; they are not intended to use interest-based advertising or remarketing. The app removes permission for the Android advertising ID and the advertising-services ID, topics and attribution APIs.

When the advertising or privacy services are used, Google may receive information such as the device’s IP address, device and app information or identifiers, approximate location inferred from the IP address, interactions with ads, and diagnostic or performance information. Google may use and share information with service providers and advertising technology partners for delivering and measuring ads, security and fraud prevention, and operating its services, subject to the applicable child and privacy settings. Google’s handling and retention are described in Google’s Privacy Policy and AdMob’s policies. We do not sell your tree creations or receive your age-group selection on a game server.

Children and families

The game is intended for a broad audience, including children. We do not ask children to create accounts or submit names, email addresses or other contact details through the game. Advertising requests for all players receive child treatment. A parent or guardian may contact us about a child’s privacy using the address below.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local game data and the saved age category stay on the device until you clear app data or uninstall the game. Delete exported images separately using the app or service where you saved them. You can contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of personal information, or to exercise other rights available where you live. Because game progress is stored locally and the game has no account, we may be unable to identify or retrieve a particular player’s local data. Google controls retention of information it processes under its own policies.

Security and changes

We use Android’s app-private storage for local game data and rely on Google’s services for advertising and privacy messages. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. We may update this policy as the game or its services change; the updated version and date will appear on this page.
