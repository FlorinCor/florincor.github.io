Santa's Run · Android

Privacy policy

How the game stores progress and how its advertising services handle data.

Effective and last updated: 7 November 2026

Who and what this policy covers

This policy covers Santa's Run, Android package com.florin.santasrun, and this policy page. The game is provided by Florin Cordunianu (FlorinCor). For privacy questions, email cordunianuflorin@gmail.com.

Game progress on your device

The game has no game account or developer game server. Route unlocks, best scores, tutorial progress and game settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. The app opts out of Android app backup. Gameplay can be used offline; Google advertising and privacy messages require an internet connection.

The game does not request your name, email address, contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. There is no separate developer-added gameplay analytics or crash-reporting service. Google advertising services have their own data practices described below.

Advertising, age settings and privacy choices

The game uses Google AdMob through the Google Mobile Ads SDK to request an interstitial ad at a transition after completing a route. Ads do not interrupt active gameplay. Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) checks privacy requirements and can show a privacy message. The game requests ads only when UMP permits it. Ad availability and privacy choices depend on your region, device and configured messages.

The game asks for a date of birth to select appropriate advertising settings. The date itself is not stored or sent off the device; only the resulting age category is stored locally. You may choose to play without ad requests instead. The game does not request ads for players who enter an age under 13. For ages 13–17, ad requests use Google's child age treatment and UMP's under-age-of-consent setting. This communicates an age-treatment signal to Google, not the birth date. All requests use a general-audience ad content rating. You can change the age category in Settings or remove it by clearing app storage.

When Google's advertising services are used, Google may collect and share IP address and approximate location, interactions with the app and ads, diagnostic and performance information, device and account identifiers such as an app-set ID and, when applicable, identifiers associated with signed-in accounts, and the age-treatment signal. Google describes these data as used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google and its advertising partners process information under their own policies and applicable privacy choices; the developer may receive aggregate advertising reports. Google states that the Mobile Ads SDK encrypts data in transit. Non-personalized ads can still involve data processing.

The app removes permission to access the Android advertising ID and Android Privacy Sandbox ad signals. If a privacy-options form is required, it can be opened from Settings → Privacy choices. Withdrawing a choice affects future processing that depends on it and does not erase information already processed.

See Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps using its services and Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Retention, deletion and security

Local game records remain until changed or removed. To remove them, use Android Settings → Apps → Santa's Run → Storage → Clear storage or uninstall the game. This removes local progress, settings and locally stored consent records. The app may ask for choices again if reopened. Device menu names may differ.

There is no game account to delete and the developer has no server copy of your game save. Clearing the app does not delete information already processed by Google or correspondence sent to the developer. Google's retention and deletion practices are described in its privacy policy and privacy controls. Google may process data in other countries. Local records rely on Android's app-private storage protections; no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Contact, rights and children

If you contact the developer, your email address, message and information you choose to provide are used to respond and handle your request. Email providers process that correspondence. Messages are retained as needed to resolve the request and maintain necessary support or legal records, then removed when no longer needed. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, object to or restrict processing, or withdraw consent. Contact the developer at cordunianuflorin@gmail.com about information handled directly by the developer, and use Google's privacy controls for information held by Google. You may also contact your local data-protection authority. A parent or guardian with a privacy concern may use the same contact address.
