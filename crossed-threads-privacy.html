Crossed Threads Privacy Policy

Effective November 20, 2026

Crossed Threads is an Android puzzle game provided by Florin Cordunianu, publishing as SimpleGamesLabs. For privacy questions or requests, email cordunianuflorin@gmail.com.

Game data and age choices

The game saves puzzle progress, move history, completed levels, and your selected age group on your device. Your choices are Under 18, 18 or older, and Prefer not to say. The game asks for an age group and does not ask for your date of birth. It does not require an account or send saved game progress or your selected group to the developer. Android may include local app data in backups according to your device settings.

You can change your age group in Settings. The game treats Prefer not to say the same as Under 18. For both choices, the game skips advertising setup and makes no advertising or advertising-consent requests. Players can use the same puzzle features regardless of the choice.

Ads and advertising privacy choices

For players who select 18 or older, the game uses Google AdMob for an app-open ad and a banner. Google’s User Messaging Platform checks whether a regional privacy message applies. Where available, Settings → Privacy and cookie settings lets you revisit the European consent choices or exercise the opt-out offered in applicable US states. Google determines ad delivery according to the applicable regional settings and choices. The game works when ads are unavailable.

When the advertising or consent service is used, Google may receive information such as IP address and the approximate location inferred from it, device or app identifiers, app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and privacy choices. Google uses this information for functions such as ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. Google and its advertising partners process information under their applicable policies. See Google’s Privacy Policy and Google’s Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Retention, deletion, and your rights

Game data and the age choice stay in app storage until you clear that storage or uninstall the game. Copies managed by Android backup services follow your backup settings. Google handles retention of data received by its services under its own policies. If you email the developer, your email address and the information you provide are used to respond. Correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and keep relevant support records.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information, withdraw consent, or opt out of certain advertising uses. To exercise a right or ask about a child’s privacy, contact cordunianuflorin@gmail.com. Where available, advertising choices can also be exercised directly in the game’s Settings. You may have the right to contact your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

The current policy and its effective date will be available on this page and through the game’s Settings.
