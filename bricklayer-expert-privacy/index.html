Bricklayer Expert · Android

Privacy policy

How the game stores your progress and how its advertising services handle data.

Effective and last updated: 9 November 2026

Who and what this covers

This policy covers Bricklayer Expert, Android package com.florin.bricklayer, and this policy page. The game is provided by Florin Cordunianu (FlorinCor). For privacy questions or requests, email cordunianuflorin@gmail.com.

The game saves progress on your device. It offers a choice of age group and can show Google AdMob interstitial ads at a break after every four completed runs. It does not request ads for players who choose an age under 18 or prefer not to say.

Game information on your device

The game has no game account or developer game server. It stores completed levels, stars, best scores, in-progress builds, tutorial progress, sound, music and haptic settings, and recovery copies in the app's private storage. It also stores your selected age group and an advertising-frequency counter. These records support play, recovery and preferences; the game does not send your saved builds or progress to a developer game server. Android app backup is disabled for this game.

You can play with the bundled game assets offline. Advertising and privacy services require an internet connection. The game does not ask for your name, email address, contacts, camera, microphone or precise location. It has no separate developer-added gameplay analytics or crash-reporting service. Google's advertising and privacy services have their own data practices described below.

Age groups and children

The game asks you to choose Under 13, 13–17, 18–24, 25–34, 35–44, 45–54, 55+, or “I prefer not to say.” It does not ask for a date of birth or identity document. Your choice is stored on the device and can be changed under Settings → Age group.

When the selected age is under 18 or unspecified, the game does not request ads. For adult selections, the game checks Google's privacy status before requesting ads. All ad requests are configured for Google's child-directed treatment and a G maximum ad-content rating; the exact selected age interval is not sent as a date of birth. A parent or guardian can contact the developer about a child's privacy using the email above.

Google advertising and privacy choices

The game uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform (UMP). After adult players complete four runs, a preloaded interstitial may appear at a natural break. If an ad is unavailable or the break is interrupted, the game continues without waiting for it. UMP checks privacy requirements and may present a consent form where needed and configured. The game requests ads only when UMP permits it. When Google requires a privacy-options entry point, Settings → Ad privacy choices lets you revisit the available choices.

When Google's advertising and privacy services are used, Google may collect and share:

  • IP address, which may indicate an approximate location;
  • app and ad interactions, such as launches, taps and video views;
  • app and SDK diagnostic or performance information; and
  • device or account identifiers, such as an app-set ID and, where applicable, identifiers associated with signed-in accounts.

Google describes these data as used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google and its advertising providers handle information under their own policies and applicable privacy choices; the developer may receive aggregate ad reports. Google says that its Mobile Ads SDK encrypts data in transit. Limited or non-personalized ads can still involve data processing.

The app removes the Android advertising-ID and Privacy Sandbox advertising permissions from its manifest and disables Google's publisher first-party ID before it explicitly requests ads. This does not mean that every device identifier is unavailable. Read Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps using its services, and Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

Retention, deletion and security

Local game records remain until changed or removed. The in-game Settings → Reset progress clears builds, scores, stars, unlocks and learned guides but retains feedback settings and your age selection. To remove all local app data, including age selection, frequency state and local privacy records, use Android Settings → Apps → Bricklayer Expert → Storage → Clear storage or uninstall the game. Device menu names may differ. Android backup is disabled.

There is no game account to delete and no developer server copy of your game save. Clearing app storage does not delete data already processed by Google or correspondence sent to the developer. Google's retention and deletion practices are described in its retention information and privacy controls. Google may process data in other countries. Local records rely on Android's app-private storage protections; no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Contact and your rights

If you contact the developer, your email address, message and information you choose to provide are used to respond and handle your request. Email providers process that correspondence. Messages are retained as needed to resolve requests and maintain necessary support or legal records. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, object to or restrict processing, or withdraw consent. Contact cordunianuflorin@gmail.com about information handled directly by the developer. For information held by Google, use Google's privacy controls. You may also contact your local data-protection authority.
