Memory Match privacy policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Memory Match is provided by Cordunianu Florin. For privacy questions or requests, email cordunianuflorin@gmail.com.

Game data

Memory Match stores game progress, records, an interrupted game and settings in app storage on your device. You do not need an account. Depending on your device settings, Android backup or device transfer may copy app data to your Google account or another device. Gameplay can be used offline.

The game asks you to select an age range at the beginning. It uses the selection to decide whether to start the advertising and consent flow. It does not ask for a date of birth. The selection is retained in the activity's saved state to handle interruptions such as rotation; you can change it in Settings. On a shared device, select the range for the current player.

Children and advertising

Memory Match is intended for children and adults. The app does not request ads or show an advertising consent form for players who select an age under 18 or prefer not to say. Players can still use the game. The Google Mobile Ads library includes an Android startup component that reads the app's ad configuration; the app starts the advertising consent flow and initializes the ad SDK only after an adult selection and the applicable consent checks.

For players who select 18 or older, the game may show a Google AdMob banner on the home screen. Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) may communicate with Google to provide ads, manage choices, measure performance, and prevent fraud. Depending on the applicable configuration, Google may process information such as IP address, approximate location derived from it, device or advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. See Google's privacy policy, how Google uses information from partner apps, and Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure. Google applies its own retention and deletion practices.

When required and available, Google's privacy form asks for choices before ads are requested. When required, you can revisit those choices through Settings → Privacy options. You can also use the advertising privacy controls in Android settings. Withdrawing consent affects future processing and does not erase information already processed. An internet connection is needed for advertising.

Deletion and your choices

You can clear completed-level progress and records in the game's settings; other settings are kept. To remove all locally stored app data, use Android's app storage settings or uninstall the game. Device and backup settings determine whether a backup remains. Clearing local app data does not delete information already processed by Google; use Google's data and privacy controls for Google-held information.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, obtain a copy of, restrict or object to the processing of personal information, or withdraw consent. Contact the developer about information the developer handles directly. You may also contact your data-protection authority. A parent or guardian may contact the developer with a concern about a child's privacy; please do not send unnecessary personal information.
