Memory Match privacy policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026

Memory Match is provided by Cordunianu Florin. For privacy questions or requests, email cordunianuflorin@gmail.com.

Game data

Memory Match stores game progress, records, an interrupted game and settings in app storage on your device. You do not need an account. Depending on your device settings, Android backup or device transfer may copy app data to your Google account or another device. Gameplay can be used offline.

The game asks you to select an age range. It uses the selection to choose the advertising and privacy treatment appropriate for the player. It does not ask for a date of birth. The selection is stored on the device and remembered when the game is reopened; you can change it in Settings or remove it by clearing app data. Android backup or device transfer may also copy this selection, depending on device settings. On a shared device, select the range for the current player.

Children and advertising

Memory Match is intended for children and adults. The game may show a Google AdMob banner at the top of level screens. For players who select an age under 18 or prefer not to say, the game configures Google's child age treatment and a G maximum ad content rating. For adult players, the game configures a PG maximum ad content rating. Google says child treatment disables personalized advertising and remarketing, prevents transmission of the Android advertising identifier, and applies child ad-serving protections. Child treatment does not mean that no data is processed. The game does not ask these players to give advertising consent. The Google Mobile Ads library includes an Android startup component that reads the app's ad configuration.

Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) may communicate with Google to provide ads, manage choices, measure performance, and prevent fraud. Depending on the player's age treatment and applicable configuration, Google may process information such as IP address, approximate location derived from it, device information, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information. For adult players, information may also include advertising identifiers. See Google's privacy policy, how Google uses information from partner apps, and Google's Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure. Google applies its own retention and deletion practices.

For adult players, when required and available, Google's privacy form asks for choices before ads are requested. When required, adults can revisit those choices through Settings → Privacy options. You can also use the advertising privacy controls in Android settings. Withdrawing consent affects future processing and does not erase information already processed. An internet connection is needed for advertising.

Deletion and your choices

You can clear completed-level progress and records in the game's settings; other settings are kept. To remove all locally stored app data, use Android's app storage settings or uninstall the game. Device and backup settings determine whether a backup remains. Clearing local app data does not delete information already processed by Google; use Google's data and privacy controls for Google-held information.

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, obtain a copy of, restrict or object to the processing of personal information, or withdraw consent. Contact the developer about information the developer handles directly. You may also contact your data-protection authority. A parent or guardian may contact the developer with a concern about a child's privacy; please do not send unnecessary personal information.
