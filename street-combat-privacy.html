Privacy Policy — Street Combat: Crossroads

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy describes the current Street Combat: Crossroads prototype for Android (application ID com.florin.streetcombat). It covers the game itself; app stores, your device, and your network provider have their own privacy practices.

Information used by the game

You can play against the CPU without creating an account. The game can run offline; an internet connection is needed to request an advertisement. The game does not send gameplay data to a developer-operated server.

The game keeps your chosen settings and match information in memory while you play. The current game code does not save your nickname, settings, or match history as a persistent game profile. Your operating system or app store may maintain its own app data, diagnostics, or records under its own policies.

Intended audience

Street Combat: Crossroads is intended for adults aged 18 and older and is not designed for children. The current prototype does not include age verification.

Advertising and privacy choices

The Android game uses Google AdMob to request an app-open ad during startup. An ad may not appear if it is unavailable, the device is offline, or the game cannot request one under the current consent status. Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) is used to request and manage applicable advertising privacy choices.

Google says its Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and shares information such as IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device or advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostics. Google may use this information for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. The SDK can also access Android advertising services for interest topics and ad attribution, where available. Depending on the AdMob configuration and your choices, other advertising partners may also receive data. See Google's privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy and its partner information at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where required, choose Privacy Options on the game's main menu to review your choices. You can also use your Android device's settings to reset or delete its advertising ID where available. Non-personalized advertising can still involve some data processing.

Nearby Fight

Nearby Fight is designed for two players on the same local Wi-Fi network. When you search or host, the game uses your device's network address and broadcasts lobby discovery information, including a temporary random lobby identifier and game protocol information, on the local network. Other devices on that network may receive these broadcasts.

When you connect, your chosen nickname is shared with the other player. The devices also exchange connection and match information such as fighter and arena selections, readiness, player inputs, positions, health, round state, and results so that the match can work. The other player can see your nickname; use a nickname that does not reveal information you wish to keep private.

A manually entered host address can connect to a reachable host outside your local network if your network permits it. The game does not use a developer-operated matchmaking server. Network administrators, the other player, or others with access to the network may be able to see network traffic.

Security and retention

Nearby connections use UDP/ENet without game-level encryption or peer authentication. Use a trusted network and avoid entering personal or sensitive information as your nickname. We cannot control what another player, their device, or network equipment retains.

Lobby and match data are used for the current session and held in game memory; the current game code does not save them as a persistent game profile. Leave the lobby or close the game to end the session. UMP may store consent information on your device. Google and advertising partners retain data under their own policies. Uninstalling the app removes its app data subject to your device's and app store's own behavior; it does not necessarily delete data held by third parties.

Permissions and sharing

The Android build uses network access for nearby connections, advertising, and privacy messages. The advertising SDK also adds permissions for network state, the Android advertising ID, Android ad services, and background processing. The game does not request camera, microphone, contacts, or device location permission. Google AdMob and UMP receive data as described above; the game does not share gameplay data with a developer-operated service.

Your choices and rights

Nearby Fight is optional; CPU mode can be played offline. You can choose a non-identifying nickname and stop a nearby session by leaving the lobby or closing the game. Depending on where you live and the circumstances, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights to access, correct, or delete it and to complain to a data protection authority. Information held independently by another player, app store, or network provider is subject to their own practices.
