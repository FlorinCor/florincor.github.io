Heart Cascade Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026
Developer: Florin Cordunianu
Android application: com.florin.heartcascade

This policy explains how information is handled by Heart Cascade, the Android puzzle game published by Florin Cordunianu, and how to contact the developer about privacy.

Heart Cascade can be played offline and does not require an account. Game progress, preferences, and your age selection are saved on your device. Google AdMob may show ads when the device is online. The type of ad depends on your age selection and applicable privacy settings. Android may back up local game data depending on your device settings.

1. Information stored by the game

Heart Cascade stores the following information in its private application storage to provide gameplay, remember your choices, and restore progress when you reopen the app:

  • Completed and unlocked levels, best scores, and stars earned.
  • In-game coin balances, boosters, and owned or selected board backdrops.
  • Daily reward progress and the date of your last reward claim.
  • Your age selection (Under 18, 18 or older, or Prefer not to say). The app uses this choice to apply advertising settings. It collects no birth date or exact age. You can review or change your selection under Settings > Age range.
  • Game statistics, sound and vibration preferences, reduced-motion settings, and whether an introductory hint has been shown, and a finished-level-attempt counter used to schedule ads.

The game uses in-game coins for its shop and requests no payment details. It does not ask for your name, email address, telephone number, location, contacts, or photos. Google may estimate a general location from your IP address. For eligible adult ad requests, Google may also use the Android advertising ID.

2. Collection, sharing, and advertising

For players who select 18 or older, an AdMob ad may appear on the loading screen when a returning player starts the game. An interstitial may appear before the results screen after every five finished level attempts. Eligible players may also opt in to rewarded ads for Hammer, Star, Rainbow, Shuffle, or five extra moves. Availability depends on the player's privacy settings and ad availability.

Players who select Under 18 or Prefer not to say receive the same advertising treatment. The level map may show a non-personalized banner using Google's child-directed ad treatment and a G content rating limit. Child-directed ad requests suppress remarketing and Android advertising ID transmission. The app sends Google the restricted age treatment signal and does not request adult consent forms or show full-screen ads for these selections. The app waits for an age selection before requesting ads.

Local saved progress and the selected age range stay on the device, subject to Android backup settings. The developer operates no player database. Google processes advertising and privacy signals as described below.

Google documents the following information collected and shared by its advertising SDK:

  • IP address, which can indicate approximate location.
  • Interaction information, such as launches, taps, and video views.
  • Diagnostics about app and SDK performance.
  • Device identifiers, including Android advertising ID and app set ID, and applicable account-related identifiers.

These data support advertising, measurement and analytics, and fraud prevention. SDK transmissions use TLS encryption. Advertising may be personalized depending on your choices and applicable settings. See Google's Mobile Ads SDK disclosure, Google's Privacy Policy, and how Google uses information from partner apps.

For players who select 18 or older, Google's User Messaging Platform displays applicable advertising privacy messages in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and supported US states. Open Settings > Ad privacy choices to review or change choices where Google provides a form. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID through your Android settings.

Google Play, Android, and your device manufacturer may separately process installation, security, backup, and diagnostic information under their own policies and your settings.

3. Android backup and transfer

Heart Cascade permits Android's system backup and device-transfer features. Depending on your Android version, device, backup provider, and settings, game progress and preferences may be copied to a backup service or another device and restored when you reinstall or transfer the app. The developer does not operate or access your Android backup. You can manage backup through your device and backup-provider settings.

4. Permissions

Heart Cascade uses the vibration permission for optional haptic feedback during play. You can turn haptics off in the game's settings. The game also declares Internet and network-state permissions for advertising, the Android advertising ID permission, and advertising-services permissions supplied by Google libraries. It does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise-location, or shared-storage access. Android libraries also use an app-specific, signature-protected permission to secure internal broadcast handling; this is not permission to collect personal information.

5. Retention and deletion

Local progress, preferences, and the selected age range remain in the app's storage until cleared or removed. To erase them, open Android Settings > Apps > Heart Cascade > Storage > Clear storage/data (the wording varies by device), or uninstall the app. Clearing storage resets your progress, coins, boosters, settings, and age selection. Resetting progress within the game preserves the age selection.

System backups may persist separately and may restore old progress after reinstallation. Manage or delete those backups through your device or backup provider. There is no Heart Cascade account or developer-hosted player database to delete. Clearing local app data does not delete information already processed by Google or advertising partners. Their retention periods and deletion controls are governed by their policies; use Google's privacy controls or contact the relevant provider for requests concerning that information.

6. Privacy enquiries

For privacy questions or requests, contact Florin Cordunianu at cordunianuflorin@gmail.com.

If you email the developer, your email address and the information you choose to include will be used to respond to your enquiry. Correspondence is retained only as needed to handle your request and any applicable obligations. You may use the same email address to request deletion of correspondence, subject to any necessary legal retention. Email providers process messages under their own privacy and retention policies. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.

7. Children

Heart Cascade treats Under 18 and Prefer not to say as choices for child-directed advertising. Parents or guardians with questions about a child's privacy or information that a child sent in a support enquiry can contact the developer using the email above.

8. Security

Game data is stored within Android's private application storage, protected by the operating system's access controls. The security of your device and any system backups also depends on your device, backup provider, and settings. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure.
