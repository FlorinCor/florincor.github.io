Christmas Present Match — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 November 2026.

About the game

Christmas Present Match is a memory game for children and adults. Gameplay works offline. Advertisements and consent services require an internet connection.

Information stored on your device

The game stores game progress, scores, unlocked levels, hints, an active game session, and settings such as sound, motion and haptics on your device. You can reset progress in Settings or remove the app and its local data through your device settings. Android backup or restore settings may affect copies maintained by the operating system.

The game asks for an age range when it starts. The game uses your selection in memory to apply age-appropriate consent settings and does not save the selection. If you prefer not to say, the game uses the same protective consent setting as for a child.

Advertising and privacy choices

The game uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to provide banner ads and manage applicable privacy choices. The game configures ads with child-directed treatment and a G content rating for all players. Google and its providers may receive and process device, network, diagnostic and advertising interaction information to deliver, measure and secure their services, subject to the settings and applicable law. The game excludes the Android advertising ID and Privacy Sandbox ad ID, attribution and Topics permissions from its manifest. Ads may be unavailable if consent or an internet connection is unavailable; the game remains playable.

Where applicable, adult users may be offered privacy choices. If Google requires a privacy-options entry point, it appears in the game's Settings. For more information about Google's data practices, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

The game is intended for a mixed audience that includes children. Child-directed ad treatment is applied to all users, and children and users who do not disclose their age are treated as under age for the consent request.
